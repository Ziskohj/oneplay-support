Frequently asked questions

Is OnePlay free?

Yes. OnePlay is a free IPTV, M3U and video player for iPhone and iPad. You bring your own Xtream Codes or M3U playlist.

Does OnePlay include channels or content?

No. OnePlay is a clean player with no built-in catalog. You add your own IPTV service or playlist.

What formats and sources does it support?

Xtream Codes, M3U/M3U8 and local files. Its own FFmpeg engine plays formats AVPlayer can't — such as MKV, AVI and HEVC — in 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision.

Is it a good alternative to TiviMate or IPTV Smarters on iPhone?

Yes. OnePlay is a free, native iPhone and iPad player with its own FFmpeg engine, EPG, catch-up, offline downloads, Chromecast and AirPlay.

Does it support EPG, catch-up, Chromecast and AirPlay?

Yes — a full EPG guide, catch-up on supported channels, Picture in Picture, Chromecast and AirPlay to your TV.
